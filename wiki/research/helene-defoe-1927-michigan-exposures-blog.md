# Helene (Defoe 1927) — Michigan Exposures blog + crew comments

## Context
User shared Michigan Exposures blog post on the first Helene (Defoe Boat & Motor Works, Bay City, MI, 1927). Page includes multiple crew oral histories from the 1950s–1970s and a live comment from Christopher Sorensen seeking photos, documents, and memories for the Cast Iron Charlie documentary. Distinct from the 1931 Bath Iron Works Helene (later USACE / Cavanaugh).
