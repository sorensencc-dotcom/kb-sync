### Where Clara Ford papers actually are [1][2]

Benson Ford Research Center, The Henry Ford, Dearborn:

- Acc. 1 Fair Lane papers subgroup, 1835–1950 (74 cu ft). Assembled from the mansion after Clara died 29 Sep 1950. Includes Clara B. Ford personal materials.
- Acc. 290 Office of Clara Ford records, 1918–1934 (6.4 cu ft). Checks, vouchers, receipts.
- Acc. 587 Office of Henry Ford and Clara Ford Estate, 1832–1955 (62.4 cu ft).
- Acc. 588 Clara Ford Estate records, 1947–1955 (2 cu ft).

These are financial/personal, not a 1945 board-fight docket. Useful for Clara/Eleanor share-threat context; not a substitute for Bugas or board minutes.

### Henry Ford II at BFRC (The Henry Ford AskUs) [3]

Official key resources:

- Acc. 2004.1.516 Henry Ford Office records
- Acc. 247 Henry Ford II Speech collection
- Acc. 975 Nevins and Hill interviews series, box 1, HFII transcripts

Secretary: Jim Cumming. Presidency dated 21 Sep 1945 after Clara and Eleanor Clay Ford pressure.

### The 1945 security reorganization file that exists [4][5]

John S. Bugas papers, 1934–1973. University of Wyoming, Collection 12525, 1.38 cu ft (2 boxes). Archives West.

Relevant folders:

- Ford Motor Company — Bennett, Harry H. files, 1945–1949
- Ford Motor Company — Politics file from J. S. Bugas, 1944–1948
- Ford Motor Company — Republican Party Contributions, 1945–1948
- Interview with George B. Heliker, 1958–1959
- April 25, 1947 industrial-relations memo

Bugas: FBI Detroit SAC → hired 1944 by Henry Ford to protect grandchildren → instrument of Bennett ouster under HFII, Sep 1945. Contemporary secondary (NYT 1978 Bugas interview; Detroit Free Press 2024) describe the Bennett confrontation and a disputed Ford-will codicil. Treat those as **hearsay until the Wyoming folders are read**.

Related, not Bentley: John E. Pokorny papers (UMich Special Collections), Bennett personnel/security assistant, 1926–1951.
