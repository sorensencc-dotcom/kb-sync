# GAP-141 Findings: Seed Rice Development – Robert L. Cheaney Claim CU-0915

## Research Summary
Primary source located and reviewed: Foreign Claims Settlement Commission of the United States, Proposed Decision, Claim No. CU-0915, Decision No. CU-2120 (approx.), Robert L. Cheaney and Marjorie L. Cheaney. Public PDF available at https://www.justice.gov/fcsc/cuba/documents/1-1500/0915.pdf (U.S. Department of Justice FCSC Cuba claims series).

## Key Corroboration from Primary Document
- Claim presented under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949, as amended, for losses from Cuban nationalizations since 1 Jan 1959.
- Total claim amount stated as $232,467.33, including personal property, business interest, experimental seed samples, debt, and cash.
- **Experimental Rice Samples**: Claimant ROBERT L. CHEANEY contended loss of three sets of experimental rice samples, valued at $150,000.00, representing five years of intensive work developing highly productive rice types with resistance to Hoja Blanca and Blast diseases, good milling/table quality, suitable for mechanical harvesters. Selections derived from crosses including Alba, Paquita, and Surinam Dima varieties; ready for multiplication program by end of 1960 but not yet named.
- Supporting evidence in the record: Claimant’s own statement (25 Aug 1967), joint affidavit of owners/stockholders of Agricola Cayamas, S.A. (15 Sep 1967), and formula for loss amount. Employment: Hired 1957 by Agricola Cayamas (large rice farm at Cayamas, Oriente) at $24,000/year + 15% interest in profits from seed sales to outside growers. Duties included technical assistance, seed quality maintenance, and new seed stock development.
- Commission finding: Agricola Cayamas, S.A. owned the three sets of experimental rice samples developed by Cheaney. Samples nationalized on 6 Dec 1961 pursuant to Law 989 (Official Gazette). Cheaney did not own the samples personally. Any value to him limited to 15% profit interest on seed sales; no such sales evidenced in the record. Claim for $150,000 rejected as based on projected future sales (contract breach, not a covered property loss under the Act). No allowance made for this item.

## Other Related Elements in the Decision
- Business interest: One-half partnership in rice farming operation (Mayajigua, Las Villas) with Rafael Capo Lemus (1959); nationalized ~15 Jan 1961. Valuation included machinery/equipment and pending rice harvest (165 acres).
- Debt: Salary owed by Agricola Cayamas ($1,250 for Dec 1960); company nationalized 17 Apr 1961.
- Personal property and other items valued and partially allowed.
