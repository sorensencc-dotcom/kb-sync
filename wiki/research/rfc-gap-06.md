### Findings Summary
# GAP-06 Findings: CIC - Ford Executive Dynamics & Politics (follow-up - Research Focus)

## Research Focus
Search administrative files from the late 1930s and early 1940s for written proof confirming whether Sorensen attempted to recruit and deploy former Detroit Police (gap card truncated at "Pol").

## Summary of Public Findings
No primary document located confirming that Charles E. Sorensen personally attempted to recruit or deploy former Detroit Police officers for Ford Motor Company roles in the late 1930s–early 1940s.

- Harry Bennett, not Sorensen, controlled the Ford Service Department (plant protection/security). Public secondary accounts and finding aids attribute recruitment of ex-boxers, ex-convicts, and informal networks to Bennett. Sorensen’s domain was production, foundry, and plant operations.
- Sorensen accompanied Bennett during the 1932 Ford Hunger March confrontation (secondary accounts, e.g., Wikipedia summary of events citing contemporary reports), but no evidence of police recruitment by Sorensen.
- Benson Ford Research Center holds Acc. 38 Charles Sorensen Papers with boxes on Employment and Sociological (boxes 59, 63, 66, 70, 72, 74, 77 covering 1929–1935). These are not digitized for public remote reading; finding aids list titles only. No published inventory entry explicitly references “Detroit police” recruitment under Sorensen.
- Henry Ford Office Papers and Sociological Department records (various accessions) reference Service Department but do not attribute police recruitment to Sorensen in public descriptions.
- Sorensen’s own memoir *My Forty Years with Ford* (1956) and contemporary coverage (Time, NYT, Automotive Hall of Fame) contain no claim or admission of recruiting former Detroit Police. He positions himself against Bennett’s influence in later years.

## Open Questions Status
- Primary accession filings / corporate archives: Acc. 38 (Sorensen Papers) and related Ford Office Papers exist at Benson Ford Research Center; contents of employment boxes not publicly readable online. No court docket located linking Sorensen to Detroit Police recruitment.
- Contradictions: Secondary literature consistently assigns Service Department personnel practices to Bennett. No primary witness account found that contradicts this by assigning recruitment to Sorensen.

## Verdict Rationale
PARTIAL. Public finding aids and secondary citations locate relevant archival series but do not surface a primary document confirming the specific claim. Default PARTIAL applied; CONFIRMED reserved for cases where a primary document was read. No invention of box contents.
