# The Henry Ford: Charles E. Sorensen Archival & Photographic Accessions

**Source Repository**: The Henry Ford / Benson Ford Research Center (Dearborn, Michigan)  
**Accession Series**: Photographic Vertical File Series (`84.1.1660`), General Photographs Series (`64.167.833`), Engineering Photographic Department (`64.167.189`), Charles C. La Croix Records (`64.167.435`), Nevins & Hill Research Notes (`64.167.572`), W.C. Cowling Records (`64.167.689`), P.E. Martin Papers (`64.167.823`).

---

## 1. Key Archival Clusters & Corroborated Evidence

### A. Willow Run Bomber Plant & WWII Aviation (1940–1945)

1. **Capt. Randolph Churchill Tour (July 24, 1942)**:
   - **Object ID**: `84.1.1660.P.189.17193` ([THF128539](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/261979)) & `64.167.189.P.17193` ([THF128541](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/494072)).
   - **Creators**: Ford Motor Company Engineering Photographic Department.
   - **Participants**: Capt. Randolph Churchill (Member of Parliament), Mead L. Bricker (Plant Manager), Charles E. Sorensen (Production Head).
   - **Significance**: Resolves negative index and date anchors for British military/parliamentary inspection during B-24 ramp-up.

2. **Senator Harry S. Truman Defense Committee Visit (April 30, 1942)**:
   - **Object ID**: `84.1.1660.P.O.2176` ([THF116362](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/180560)).
   - **Participants**: Senator Harry S. Truman (Senate Special Committee to Investigate the National Defense Program), Charles E. Sorensen.
   - **Significance**: Primary photographic record of Truman's initial investigation of Willow Run production bottlenecks and tooling controversies.

3. **President Franklin D. Roosevelt Tour (September 18, 1942)**:
   - **Object ID**: `64.167.435.P.833.77104.9` ([THF93105](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/62490)), `84.1.1660.P.833.77104.9` ([THF120796](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/440210)), `84.1.1660.P.O.994` ([THF120810](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/219190)).
   - **Participants**: President Franklin D. Roosevelt, Eleanor Roosevelt, Henry Ford, Edsel Ford, Charles Sorensen.
   - **Vehicle**: Presidential 1939 Lincoln limousine (*Sunshine Special*).

4. **1:98 Scale Model Layout & Production Planning (July 1941)**:
   - **Object ID**: `64.167.833.P.75904` ([THF114380](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/359145)).
   - **Significance**: Documentation of Sorensen and engineering staff establishing the L-shaped twin parallel moving lines and 90-degree turntables.

5. **Willow Run Groundbreaking Ceremony (September 17, 1940)**:
   - **Object ID**: `64.167.833.P.74367.A` ([THF127227](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/494731)), `84.1.1660.P.833.74367.A` ([THF127223](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/136240)).
   - **Participants**: Edsel Ford, P.E. Martin, Charles Sorensen.

6. **Igor Sikorsky VS-300 Helicopter Donation (October 7, 1943)**:
   - **Object ID**: `84.1.1660.P.833.78778.3` ([THF118090](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/99039)), `64.167.833.P.78778.13` ([THF118138](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/301870)).
   - **Significance**: Sorensen filmed the presentation while directing B-24 Liberator aviation operations.

7. **Final M-4A3 Tank at Rouge Plant (September 15, 1943)**:
   - **Object ID**: `P.833.78658.1` ([THF93756](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/371858)).
   - **Participants**: Charles Sorensen, Henry Ford II, and company officials.
