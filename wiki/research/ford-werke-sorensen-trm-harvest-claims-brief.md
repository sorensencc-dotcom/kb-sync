## What changes documentary treatment decisions

Only items that should change how CIC treats Sorensen knowledge, dual-track ops, or forced-labor timeline:

1. **Do not treat Acc. 65 as AR-65-1500.** Acc. 65 = Owen W. Bombard Interviews (HFM). Wartime Germany file used by Research Findings is **FMC AR-65-1500, Box 6, File: Germany 1939–1945**. Mislabeling collapses oral-history Acc. 65 into Dearborn wartime correspondence. (FW-001, FW-002)

2. **Acc. 38 Box 85 “military vehicle cables” is UNCONFIRMED against the finding aid.** Box 85 folder titles are Accident Reports … European Purchases (5 folders). Research Findings itself cites Box 85 only for *European Purchase 1938 – Group 5* (16 Apr 1938 Sorensen→Dearborn), not “military vehicle cables” or Göring motor mail. Box 40 has Germany General (2) + Minutes 1937–1938 (2) only. (FW-004–FW-008)

3. **Acc. 38 Foreign Ford Companies ends 1929–1939.** Germany 1940–1945 control papers are outside that subsubseries scope; RF points wartime letters to AR-65-1500 / other FMC accessions. Do not imply Acc. 38 Foreign Ford holds the 1939–45 Schmidt–Sorensen file. (FW-007)

4. **FCSC: award $785,321 (5 Apr 1967) after $522,526 reduction — payment NOT confirmed.** Research Findings explicitly: “Research to date has not confirmed when or how much of the award was paid.” Do not narrate $522k/$785k as *disbursed* without a Treasury/FCSC payment schedule. (FW-024–FW-026)

5. **Forced-labor peak and Buchenwald timing are decision-stable from RF + memorial site; Dearborn knowledge of that labor is not.** RF: foreign workers 314 (Apr 1942) → 1,932 (Aug 1944); Buchenwald detachment from Aug 1944 (~50 at a time, ≥65 assigned). Buchenwald aussenlager page confirms Köln-Niehl Ford. RF summary claims parent–subsidiary communication ceased by Nov 1941 *before* forced/slave labor — present as RF’s conclusion until AR-65-1500 and Lawler letters are primary-read. (FW-014, FW-018–FW-019, FW-029)

Schneider Box 1032 and Lawler remain **catalog-level / secondary** until exhibit pages and letter scans are reopened — do not invent exhibit text. (FW-009–FW-013)
