## Target 1: Ernest G. Liebold papers
- **Accession**: Acc. 64 (also referenced as 64.167 series components)
- **Dates**: 1906-1951 (bulk 1918-1933)
- **Extent**: 0.4 cubic ft
- **Repository**: Benson Ford Research Center, The Henry Ford
- **Description (from ArchiveGrid / BFRC finding-aid summaries)**: Primarily copied documents offered by Liebold after 1950s oral-history interviews. Original material limited to bank books (1918-1927) as treasurer of Dearborn Publishing Company. Includes:
  - Correspondence 1925-1928 re: transfer of Edison’s Fort Myers laboratory to Dearborn
  - 1912 Ford-Edison storage-battery agreement copy
  - 1927 letter of apology to Louis Marshall (American Jewish Committee)
  - Diary of 1930 European trip with Henry Ford
  - Light’s Golden Jubilee transportation arrangements (1929)
  - 1927 South American rubber plantation documents
  - 1933 Viereck interview with Henry Ford
  - A.Y. Malcolmson interview summary (1919)
  - Muscle Shoals hydroelectric report (undated)
- **Related**: Ernest G. Liebold oral history Acc. 65
- **Access**: Open for research
- **Citation form**: Item, folder, box, Accession 64, Ernest G. Liebold papers, Benson Ford Research Center, The Henry Ford

## Target 2: Frank Campsall Files / Records series
- **Accession**: SE-007 (also Acc. 292 in some series descriptions)
- **Title**: Frank Campsall records series, 1887-1947 (bulk 1920-1935)
- **Extent**: 19.2 cubic ft. + 8 oversize boxes
- **Repository**: Benson Ford Research Center, The Henry Ford
- **Subseries (public finding-aid summary)**:
  - Wayside Inn, Sudbury, Mass. records (1903-1938, 11.6 cf) – largest component; correspondence with E.J. Boyer, restoration progress reports, blueprints, antique acquisition
  - Henry Ford Trips and Interests records (1912-1947, 2.4 cf)
  - Additional subseries covering personal interests managed by Campsall
- **Biographical note**: Frank Charles Campsall (1884-1946), personal secretary/confidant to Henry Ford from ~1912; oversaw details of Ford personal projects; traveled with Fords.
- **Access**: Open for research; box list and finding aid available (PDF referenced: FrankCampsallRecordsSeries_AccessionSE007.pdf on dalnet.lib.mi.us)
- **Citation form**: Item, folder, box, accession no., Frank Campsall records series, Personal Topics Subject File subgroup, Henry Ford Office records, Benson Ford Research Center, The Henry Ford
