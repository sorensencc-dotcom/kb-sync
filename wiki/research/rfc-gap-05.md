Both accessions exist at BFRC and are open. Neither is a 1943–45 executive-politics docket. Acc. 823 is a small early-production personal file that ends before the succession fight. Acc. ### Acc. 823 — P. E. Martin papers [1]

- Creator: Peter Edmund Martin, 1882–1944. Early FMC production executive; Sorensen’s senior at Highland Park / Rouge in the Model T years.
- Dates: 1880–1943. Quantity: **1.2 cubic ft. + 1 oversize box.** Open.
- Series: Photographs 1880–1943 (0.8 cu ft + OS); Papers 1922–1941 (0.4 cu ft).
- Papers contents per finding aid: 1930 correspondence; production totals Model A (1903) through late Model A (1928); late-1920s personal notebook (Stout, Tri-Motor, Rouge, Highland Park, payroll, Trade School, glass/steel, Model T/A figures).
- THF AskUs also points here as the Martin key resource.

**Use:** early production hierarchy and who sat where relative to Sorensen. **Do not use** as a 1943–45 politics file. Martin is dead in 1944; the papers stop at 1941/43.

Finding aid: https://www.dalnet.org/henryford/docs/PEMartinPapers_Accession823.pdf

### Acc. SE-007 — Frank Campsall records series [2][3]

- Creator: Frank Charles Campsall, 1884–1946. Personal secretary to Henry Ford from Highland Park purchasing (1912) through 1946. Prepared and handled signing of Henry Ford’s **1945 resignation letter** when HFII became president (THF AskUs, Campsall entry).
- Dates: 1887–1947, **bulk 1920–1935**. Quantity: **19.2 cu ft + 8 oversize boxes.** Open. Part of Personal Topics Subject File subgroup, Henry Ford Office records.
- Largest chunk: Wayside Inn, Sudbury, Acc. 292 nested (11.6 cu ft). Also properties (Savannah, La Belle, Stratford prospect), Dearborn Country Club, Henry Ford Hospital, 1928 England trip, Scotch Settlement School.
- Died 16 Mar 1946, Savannah hospital, on a trip with the Fords.

Finding aid PDF: http://www.dalnet.lib.mi.us/henryford/docs/FrankCampsallRecordsSeries_AccessionSE007.pdf

**Use:** who was in the room for Henry’s personal decisions; the 1945 resignation package; late 1943–45 correspondence if any survived outside the 1920–35 bulk. Ask staff specifically for 1943–45 folders before requesting the Wayside Inn mountain.
