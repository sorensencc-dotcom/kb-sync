**Obituary Clipping (“Automobile Pioneer Dies At 86”)**
- Died Tuesday at home in Bethesda, Md., after long illness, age 86. (Aligns with 13 Aug 1968.)
- Native of Copenhagen; arrived America with parents at age 4.
- Son of a model-maker who worked in wood.
- Joined Ford 1905 as $3/day pattern-maker in two-story wooden building on Detroit’s upper East Side.
- By 4 March 1944 resignation, Ford assets exceeded $1 billion.
- Authored *My Forty Years with Ford* (1957). Knighted by King Christian of Denmark.
- Designed Willow Run bomber plant producing B-24 Liberators at one per working hour.
- Married Helen Mitchell of Detroit 1904; son Clifford (deceased). Married Mrs. Thompson Montgomery of Washington 1960.
- Survivors: two sisters (Detroit area), two grandchildren.
- Services at Community Church, Miami Beach (vestryman). Burial in Miami.
