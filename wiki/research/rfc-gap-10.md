## Summary of Public Finding Aids & Secondary Corroboration
VERIFIED via public finding aids (Benson Ford Research Center / The Henry Ford):

- **Samuel Simpson Marquis papers**: Accession 63 (correspondence, clippings, notebooks, photographs, publications, speeches re: Peace Ship; microfilm of correspondence/reports on profit-sharing, Educational/Sociological Dept, Peace Ship, and *Henry Ford: An Interpretation*). Accession 293 (report on Ford profit-sharing plan; 210-page transcript of Marquis's 1920 court testimony describing laborer classification and wage rates at Ford Motor Company). Portion of Sociological Dept microfilm available in paper as Accession 1018.
- Marquis (b. 1866 Sharon OH; Episcopalian minister; rector St. Joseph's Detroit 1901; dean St. Paul's Cathedral Detroit from 1906) joined Sociological Dept 1915 at Henry Ford's invitation; accompanied Peace Ship; became head after John R. Lee; resigned 21/25 January 1921 amid declining Ford support for sociological work. Published critical *Henry Ford: An Interpretation* (1923).
- Related: Accession 1283 (Sociological Dept; Marquis correspondence to J.D. Oliver); Henry Ford Office Papers (Acc. 62, 285) contain Sociological Dept reports/correspondence 1920s.
- Cranbrook Archives hold additional Marquis personal papers including correspondence series 1914–1935 and Ford Welfare Dept series (speeches, writings, 1920 wage arbitration testimony).
