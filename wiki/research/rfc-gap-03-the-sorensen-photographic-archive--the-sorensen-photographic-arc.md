# RFC: GAP-03 — Capt. Randolph Churchill Willow Run Negative Indexing

## 1. Problem Statement & Context
Locate the specific Ford photographic negative catalog numbers and exact date for British Member of Parliament Capt. Randolph Churchill's visit to Willow Run Bomber Plant with Charles Sorensen and Mead Bricker during summer 1942.

## 2. Evidence Grounding & Primary Accessions

### Corroborated Archival Evidence
- **Date Verified**: **July 24, 1942**
- **Negative / Object IDs**:
  - `84.1.1660.P.189.17193` ([THF128539](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/261979)): Photographic Vertical File Series.
  - `64.167.189.P.17193` ([THF128541](https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/494072)): Photographs for the Company Series.
- **Photographic Department**: Ford Motor Company Engineering Photographic Department.
- **Participants**: Capt. Randolph Churchill (center/right), Charles E. Sorensen (center), Mead L. Bricker (left).

## 3. Resolution & Knowledge Base Grounding
The negative reference `P.189.17193` is confirmed in the collections of The Henry Ford / Benson Ford Research Center. This resolves the chronological uncertainty and ties the British parliamentary inspection directly to the July 24, 1942 B-24 manufacturing tour.
