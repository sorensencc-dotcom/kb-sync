# CIC Archival Audit & Cork Operations Investigation

Resolved death date (13 Aug 1968), closed FCSC Cuban claims hypothesis, logged Willow Run production totals (8,685), and extracted the full primary timeline. ## Cork Operations Investigation

- **Incorporation & Site**: Incorporated 17 April 1917; first purpose-built Ford plant outside North America (Marina, former racecourse, ~330,000 sq ft).
- **Motivation**: Ford ancestry (Ballinascarthy / Fair Lane) + 1912 visit + WWI tractor demand.
- **Production**: First Fordson 3 July 1919 (303 in 1919; 3,626 in 1920; ~7,605 total 1919–22). Ceased end-1922; sole global Fordson source 1929–1932. Shifted to vehicle assembly post-1932.
- **Peak Employment**: ~7,000 (c. 1930).
- **Sorensen's Role**: Directed Irish operations ~20+ years; extensive BFRC Acc. 38 correspondence; regular visits; set policy on production, wages, tariffs. Completely omitted from 1956 memoir.
- **Challenges**: Operated through War of Independence and Civil War (Grace kept plant open; unharmed in Burning of Cork). McKenna tariffs initially advantageous; post-Treaty ~22.2% tariff on parts to UK undermined model.
- **Closure**: 13 July 1984 after EEC tariff phase-out.
- **Key Sources**: BFRC Acc. 38 & Acc. 266; Grimes PhD thesis (Maynooth 2008); Henry Ford Research Guide; Century Ireland / Blaney; Irish Examiner centenary series.
