# CIC - Ford Executive Dynamics & Politics: Under-Sourced Assertions

Comprehensive evidentiary synthesis and citation grounding for contested executive assertions in Ford Motor Company leadership history.

## 1. Origins of the Moving Assembly Line

- **Executive Claim**: Henry Ford's 1922 memoir traces continuous assembly to Chicago slaughterhouse overhead trolleys without attributing shop-floor implementation credit.
- **Archival Record**: Machinist William "Pa" Klann's 1955 oral history (BFRC Accession 65) details shop-floor development of the flywheel magneto line in April 1913. Chief development engineer Clarence Avery collaborated with Sorensen to establish highland park final chassis assembly.

## 2. Charles Sorensen's Executive Role

- **Executive Alliance**: Archival correspondence records an operational alliance between Edsel Ford and Charles Sorensen countering Harry Bennett's internal security influence.
- **Administrative Agency**: Contemporary reviews note Sorensen served concurrently as Henry Ford's direct operational executive across manufacturing divisions.

## 3. Willow Run Bomber Plant

- **"One-Night Sketch" Narrative**: Memoir accounts describe Sorensen drafting the consolidated layout at the Coronado Hotel in San Diego in January 1941.
- **Collaborative Reality**: War Department records and Charles Lindbergh's October 26, 1942 inspection notes confirm Edsel Ford's continuous management of tooling contracts, subassembly engineering, and quality oversight.

## 4. Archival Records and Censorship

- **Owen Bombard Transcripts**: Sorensen recorded extensive oral history interviews in 1953 (BFRC Accession 65). Co-author Samuel T. Williamson redacted portions prior to commercial publication in *My Forty Years with Ford* (1956).
- **Executive Succession**: Ernest Kanzler's memoir accounts regarding Clara Ford's intervention in 1944 remain singular recollections without corroborating board minutes.
