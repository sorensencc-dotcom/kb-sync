# WikiConceptsTRMClosedLoopResearch

## Summary
Synthesized documentation node for the Topic Research Matrix (TRM) closed-loop research pipeline.

## Architectural Overview
The TRM closed-loop research pipeline ingests structured research payloads, applies SHA-256 integrity verification, enforces path traversal safeguards, and synthesizes structured wiki nodes into local knowledge stores and the SQLite context cache.
